What Mentor does

Think of it less like software and more like a capable operations lead that never sleeps — one that works inside your rules.

Coordinates

Routes work, chases the follow-ups, and keeps the moving parts in sync so nothing slips.

Remembers

Builds lasting memory of your customers, jobs, quirks, and what worked last time — institutional knowledge that sticks.

Escalates

Handles the routine on its own and brings the important decisions to the right person, fast.

Reasons

Weighs options against what your business actually cares about — not just the loudest request.

Learns

Spots the recurring problems and fixes the cause, so the same issue stops coming back.

Stays in its lane

Acts only within the limits you set, logs why it did everything, and never crosses your bright lines.

Why it's different

Built around intent, not forms

You tell Mentor what the business is trying to achieve. It works backwards from that, instead of leaving you to drive every screen.

You set the rules; trust is earned

Everything starts cautious. The AI only earns more independence as it proves itself — and your hard limits are absolute.

People stay in the loop

Mentor is built to reduce the noise and surface the decisions, not to take humans out of the picture.

No lock-in, future-proof

It runs on whatever AI engine you choose, and the whole operating model is plain Markdown — readable, portable, yours.
