Mentor / Organisation Map

Clean Hierarchy

A calm, top-down structure for Mentor. The layers above Mentor define the goals, permissions and learning loops. Mentor Core then coordinates the agent domains, property clusters and human approvals below it.

Level 1

Strategic Layer

The business intent: guest experience, owner satisfaction, revenue goals, growth priorities and operational philosophy.

contains
Guest ExperienceProtect reviews, reduce friction and help guests faster.
Revenue GoalsOptimise occupancy, rates, owner returns and gap-fill opportunities.
GrowthScale the business without adding unnecessary operational chaos.
EfficiencyReduce interruptions, manual chasing and repeated mistakes.
Level 2

Governance Layer

The safety rails: what Mentor may do automatically, what needs approval, who gets notified, and where human judgement is required.

controls
Approval RulesRefunds, owner messages, contractor calls and sensitive responses.
AI PermissionsDefines what agents can do without asking first.
Escalation PoliciesEmergency handling, after-hours rules and priority thresholds.
Compliance & SafetyKeeps decisions explainable, auditable and appropriate.
Level 3

Learning & Simulation Layer

The improvement loop: Mentor learns from patterns, tests likely outcomes and suggests better workflows over time.

improves
Pattern DetectionFinds repeated guest issues, property quirks and workflow failures.
Prediction EngineForecasts review risk, late cleans, booking gaps and operational pressure.
Workflow OptimisationSuggests better SOPs, templates and cleaner checklists.
Simulation EngineCompares possible actions before Mentor recommends one.
Level 4
Core Intelligence Layer

Mentor Core

Central reasoning engine coordinating memory, agents, workflows, approvals, communication and operational intelligence across Akaroa Holiday Homes.

Why this worksThe top layers define intent and limits. Mentor does not just act — it acts within business goals and governance rules.
How it scalesNew agents, properties and workflows slot into the lower layers without changing the strategic structure above.
Where humans fitHumans are not replaced. They become approval points, escalation points and source-of-truth contributors.
