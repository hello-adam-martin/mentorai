# Approvers

Who can authorise actions. One sub-folder per approver; its node file holds their channels and
scope. Approve-tier actions name an approver here.

| approver (folder) | role |
| --- | --- |
| `Approver1` | `<role>` — `<what they can approve>` |

Add an approver by copying `Approver1/` and editing its node file.
