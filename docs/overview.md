# Mentor
## The Autonomous Operations Platform

---

# Vision

Mentor is an autonomous operational intelligence platform designed to help businesses coordinate people, workflows, communication, systems, memory, and decision-making through a hierarchy of AI agents.

Unlike traditional business software, Mentor is not built around tables, forms, or dashboards.

Mentor is built around:
- intent
- coordination
- reasoning
- operational awareness
- human collaboration
- continuous learning

The goal is to create a system that feels less like software and more like a living operational nervous system.

---

Traditional software:

Mentor should:

# The Mentor Hierarchy

The platform is structured as a layered hierarchy.

This hierarchy is critical.

The layers above define:
- goals
- permissions
- constraints
- learning

The layers below perform:
- execution
- coordination
- operational work

---

# Layer 1 — Strategic Layer

The strategic layer defines the business intent.

Examples:
- maximise guest satisfaction
- reduce operational interruptions
- improve owner retention
- increase occupancy
- reduce labour overhead
- maintain premium experience

This layer answers:

```text
What is the business trying to achieve?
```

The strategic layer influences:
- prioritisation
- decision weighting
- escalation logic
- operational behaviour
- AI recommendations

---

# Layer 2 — Governance Layer

The governance layer defines:
- permissions
- approval boundaries
- AI autonomy
- escalation policies
- compliance rules
- trust boundaries

Examples:

```text
AI can:
✓ send guest replies
✓ create tasks
✓ coordinate cleaners

AI cannot:
✗ issue large refunds
✗ modify owner payouts
✗ contact media
✗ change pricing rules without approval
```

This layer is essential.

Without governance:
- the platform becomes dangerous
- trust breaks down
- businesses lose confidence

---

# Layer 3 — Learning & Simulation Layer

This is where Mentor evolves.

Responsibilities:
- pattern detection
- workflow optimisation
- simulation
- prediction
- recommendation generation
- operational improvement

Examples:

```text
Repeated issue:
Guests confused about parking.

Mentor recommends:
- updating check-in guide
- adding annotated arrival photos
- improving signage
```

Simulation examples:

```text
If early check-in approved:
+ guest satisfaction
- cleaner pressure

If denied:
+ operational stability
- review risk
```

This layer transforms Mentor from:

```text
automation
```

into:

```text
adaptive operational intelligence
```

---

Responsibilities:

---

## Operational Memory

Mentor should remember:
- recurring issues
- owner preferences
- guest patterns
- contractor history
- operational quirks
- workflow outcomes

Examples:

```text
Property:
French Bay Retreat
```


Mentor should understand stress.

- multiple incidents
- delayed turnovers
- unhappy guests
- rising review risk
- contractor shortages

The system should visually and operationally prioritise pressure areas.

## Human-in-the-Loop AI

Mentor should support varying autonomy levels.

Examples:

```text

```

This makes Mentor usable in real businesses.
